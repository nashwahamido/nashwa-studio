About Me

Who am I?

I started in graphic design and grew into senior marketing art for studios like Meta, YAGER and FunRock, making key art, trailers, cinematics and ad campaigns. During my MSc in Interactive Digital Media at Trinity College Dublin I moved into building products – as project manager and full-stack developer on MODU, an accessible 3D assembly app, and on Atlasphere, a real-time trip-planning web app. I work where art and technology meet, with a focus on making things clear and accessible.
